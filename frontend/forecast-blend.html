Forecast-model blend

Two learned weights per weather model — one for wind speed, one for wind direction — fitted so the blended 10 m wind matches ERA5 reanalysis as closely as possible over the corridor. Speed is blended directly; direction is blended through unit vectors, so 350° and 10° average to 0°, never 180°. The live Current and Detailed wind pages apply these weights to today’s forecasts.

Learned weights & held-out accuracy

Weights are constrained to the probability simplex (non-negative, each set sums to 1), so a weight reads directly as “how much to trust this model”. Fit on the first 75% of the record; every score below comes from the final 25% the fit never saw.
